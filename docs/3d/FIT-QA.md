# FIT-QA: does every accessory that is shown look physically fitted?

Generated by `backend/scripts/fit-qa.ts` + `fit-qa-report.ts` (real viewer code, headless Chrome, each car's runtime config from the dev DB). Fit versions: rims 8, wings / bumpers / other 4.

## Tiers

- **GOOD (shown)**: visible clip ≤ 6 mm and visible gap ≤ 6 mm, or ≤ 0.5 % of the part's size when that is larger (size = the largest side of one copy: the E36 bumper → 9 mm, the E36 wing → 6.5 mm, a rim → 6 mm).
- **PERFECT**: clip ≤ 2 mm and gap ≤ 3 mm (a label in this report and in the admin fit info; customers see GOOD and PERFECT the same).
- **REFUSED**: above GOOD; not shown, with the measured reason.

## What is measured

- **Clip**: ~2 400 samples spread over the whole placed part (area-weighted). A sample is behind the car when it lies behind the nearest car surface (by that surface's own normal). It counts only where it is **visible**: next to a place where the part is in front of that surface (a crossing) that is not at the open edge of a panel (the part tucked under a fender / grille-panel edge, as bumpers are built), and on car surface a viewer outside can see (the part itself hides crossings more than 5 cm away; a crossing inside a wheel well behind the tyre does not count). A part more than half buried counts its deepest point.
- **Gap**: samples within 15 mm of the car form contact clusters; a cluster that never touches floats by its closest point; a touching cluster is cut into 5 cm slices along its contact footprint (≤ 6 mm) and every slice must touch too — a lifting corner or heel counts, not only the measured feet. Wheels never count as contact. Rims: the gap is the rim lip in its tyre (built tyres sit 12 mm over it); the wheel-to-arch gap is meant to be there.
- **Auto-correct** (GOOD first, then as close to PERFECT as it gets): moves of 2–10 mm along one axis (a wing's base plate seats down / up to 10 mm), then the two best together, then scale (±2 %, bumpers ±3 %); bumpers then bend each corner on its own (x / y / z up to 15 mm, fading in from 40 % of the half width). Rims are measured, never moved (their arch / brake rules place them).
- **Bumpers** hide, with the stock bumper, the small stock pieces sitting in / on its space (headlight washers, brackets, fillers: every piece of the mesh < 20 cm and inside the stock bumper's box + 5 cm; not lights, glass, wheels or mechanical parts).
- **Grounding**: every car stands on its real lowest point (the tyres on all 21 cars). Before, a loose bounding box (rotated meshes) left the Fabia 198 mm, the Corolla E170 80 mm and the E36 12 mm in the air.
- **Stock clip** = the car's own part (stock bumper / wheels) measured the same way: when it clips as deep, the refusal is put on the car model. **Seams**: soft dark ribbons along every contact cluster; rims a soft ring at the rim lip and an arch shadow.
- Stand-ins (`QA_STANDIN_*`, generated boxes: rear bumper, front lip, rear diffuser, side skirt, roof rack) are **test only**, never published: they only say whether a TYPE can be placed on a car. A part made for another car is never offered there; it is fitted for diagnosis only. Neither counts in the main number.

## Findings

- **Real parts: all shown.** The E36 front bumper (GOOD 2.2 mm clip / 9.0 mm gap, limit 9 mm) and the E36 wing (GOOD 2.3 / 5.0, limit 6.5) show on the E36; the rim shows on all 21 cars (PERFECT). Before the tiers: bumper 43.3 mm clip + 12.8 mm gap, wing 7 mm gap.
- **Bumper**: the two stock pieces inside its space (headlight washers, one small bracket) are hidden with the stock bumper; the 43 mm corner clip was mostly the stock `bumper_R_trim` and the grille panel edge, now treated as tucked-under; the remaining correction is scale 0.98, 8 mm up, left corner -10 mm y, right corner -10 mm x / -10 mm z. The headlight gap fell from 12.8 mm to 9.0 mm: it stays the widest spot (the part's top edge vs the stock bumper line), still within 0.5 % of the part width.
- **Wing**: seats 2 mm down; its 5 mm rear-edge gap is the Meshy surface (GOOD, not PERFECT).
- **Stand-ins are rigid boxes** (test only): refusals are mostly gaps where a straight box meets a curved car. They measure type coverage, not quality.
- **Grounding**: every car now stands on its real lowest point (the tyres). The Fabia (198 mm) and Corolla E170 (80 mm) are no longer in the air; see the gate table.
- **Part maps**: only the E36 is confirmed; bumpers are not offered on other cars until their map is confirmed.
